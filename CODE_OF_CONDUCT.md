# Code of conduct

Be straightforward and do not be a jerk. That covers almost everything.

Concretely, in issues, pull requests and reviews:

- Criticise the code, the design, or the number. Not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before explaining why
  they were wrong.
- No harassment, no slurs, no doxxing, and no bringing someone's wallet or
  losses into a technical argument.
- Do not use this repository to promote a token, yours or anyone else's.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or delete anything that ignores the above, and may
block accounts that keep doing it. There is no appeals process; there are four
repositories and one maintainer.

Report problems to `conduct@shellr.trade`.
